- License: CC0 1.0, as stated on the model's source page; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Local source: `sports_car.glb`, SHA-256 `bbb1c718d2aaf5f4344e9fb2cd66d8332a998a515b09ddd4dfa14698d787124e`.
- The file was obtained via the GitHub mirror [Shaikh-Saniya/3D-RACING-DODGE-GAME](https://github.com/Shaikh-Saniya/3D-RACING-DODGE-GAME/blob/main/Sports%20Car%20by%20Quaternius%20-%201mkmFkAz5v.glb).
- `tools/import_quaternius.py` exports the body geometry as OBJ, scales and positions it for this mod, maps materials to our own textures, and omits the source wheels. The game continues to use its separate animated wheels.
